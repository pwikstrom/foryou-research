How to get your personal data from TikTok

Getting your data is super easy! Request it from TikTok, wait until it's ready to download (usually a few seconds, sometimes a few hours), download the file, and upload it here. You can do this in the TikTok app or in a browser at tiktok.com.

  1. In the TikTok app, tap Profile, open the menu (☰) and choose Settings and privacy — or on tiktok.com, click your profile picture and choose Settings.
  2. Go to Account → Download your data (on the website, look under Privacy).
  3. Select All data (or at least your Activity) and choose JSON as the file format, then tap Request data.
  4. When your file appears under the Download data tab, download the .zip.
  5. Unzip it and upload the user_data_tiktok.json inside. You review everything before it is shared.

How to get your personal data from Instagram

Instagram data is requested through Meta's Accounts Centre. It usually takes from a few minutes up to a day before your file is ready.

  1. In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap the menu (☰), then Your activity → Download your information — or go to Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions in a browser.
  2. Choose Download or transfer information and select your Instagram account.
  3. Pick All available information (simplest), or "Some of your information" with at least Likes, Story interactions and Ads information selected.
  4. Choose Download to device, set Date range to All time and Format to JSON, then create the file.
  5. Instagram notifies you when the .zip is ready. Download it and upload it here as-is — no need to unzip. Only the activity files are read, and you review everything before it is shared.

How to get your personal data from YouTube

YouTube data comes through Google Takeout. The export usually arrives within minutes, occasionally hours.

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account you use for YouTube.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Click All YouTube data included and keep at least history, comments and playlists.
  4. Click Multiple formats and set History to JSON.
  5. Continue with Export once and create the export. Google emails you a download link when it is ready.
  6. Download the .zip and upload it here as-is — no need to unzip. Only your watch history and activity files are read, and you review everything before it is shared.

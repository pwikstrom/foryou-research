What Shapes Your TikTok Feed?

The For You Research Project examines TikTok's recommendation algorithm and its effects on Australian audiences, and how it influences what Australians see and create on the platform.

The research questions

What is recommended?

What content does TikTok recommend to Australian users, and how does this shape culture?

How is it experienced?

How do different people experience and interpret the algorithm in their everyday use of the platform?

How do creators adapt?

How do TikTok creators adapt their strategies to stay visible and relevant?

How we study it

The project combines data analysis, interviews and user participation across three research streams:

Platform analysis

Large-scale analysis of content and recommendation trends. The Data Hub supports this stream, turning shared feed data into analysable datasets.

User experiences

Interviews and data donations from everyday users, exploring how people experience and make sense of algorithmic recommendation.

Creator strategies

Working with content creators of all sizes to understand how they navigate visibility on the platform.

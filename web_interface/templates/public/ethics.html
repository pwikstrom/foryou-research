This is the same participant information and consent statement you are shown before you share a data file, reproduced here so you can read it at any time — before you decide, or long after.

Ethics approval

The For You Research Project is conducted under approval from the QUT Human Research Ethics Committee, approval number LR 2024-8002-21442. The project is led from QUT's Digital Media Research Centre with the University of Sydney, and funded by the Australian Research Council (grant DP240102939). The About page lists the investigators.

Withdrawing data you have already shared

You can withdraw a collection you have shared at any time from My stuff → My Collections in the Hub, or by emailing the team. Withdrawal removes the collection from the corpus and from any study built on it.

Account terms are separate and cover your Hub login rather than your data: see the terms of use.

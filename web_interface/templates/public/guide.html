Using the Data Hub

A step-by-step guide to getting access and running your first analysis session.

Getting access

  1. Create an account. Go to the sign-up page and register with your email address, a username and a password.
  2. Wait for approval. New accounts are reviewed by an administrator before they can log in. You'll see "pending approval" until your account is activated — if it takes more than a few days, email foryou@qut.edu.au.
  3. Log in. Your role determines which parts of the hub you can see — most researchers get the analysis tabs; data ingestion and administration are restricted.

Your first session

  1. Pick a study. Everything in the hub operates on the active study — a defined dataset combining selected data collections and a date range. Choose one from the dropdown in the header; the studies you can access are listed under My stuff → My Studies.
  2. Get an overview in Explore. Open the Explore tab to see how any variable is distributed across the study. Use the filter panel to slice the data — for example, videos from a particular content category, or plays longer than a threshold — and switch to two-slice mode to compare groups side by side. Significance stars flag differences between the slices.
  3. Follow a participant in Timelines. Pick a collection (one participant's donation) and watch how their feed composition evolves day by day. Filter chips isolate rising, falling, spiking or volatile categories, and "Show Findings" lists detected trends, anomalies and structural breaks.
  4. Drill into videos. From any chart in Explore or Correlations, click through to Video Analysis to watch the underlying videos, inspect their full metadata and AI annotations, and add your own tags and notes.
  5. Go deeper. Correlations charts relationships between numeric measures (with regression lines, group ellipses and a correlation heatmap), and Semantic Space maps every annotated video by meaning so you can explore the corpus's micro-genres.

The tabs at a glance

Explore

Distribution comparison across the active study. Numeric variables render as histograms with means; categorical variables as stacked bars. Two-slice mode runs two filtered selections side by side with significance testing.

Timelines

One participant's activity over time. Smoothed per-variable timelines with optional raw daily values, trend/anomaly/break detection, and per-day drill-down stats.

Video Analysis

Browse, watch and tag individual videos. Filter on any metadata variable, step through the result set, inspect the full enrichment record, and annotate with open tags, closed schemes and notes.

Correlations

Scatter plots (one dot per collection-day) on any two numeric axes with group ellipses and regression stats, plus a Pearson correlation heatmap across all numeric variables.

Semantic Space

A 2D map of every annotated video positioned by meaning, grouped into auto-named niches. Colour by niche, category or popularity; click any dot to open the video.

My stuff

Your personal pages: the studies you can access, your coding tasks, preferences (theme, sharing, variable customizations), your video tags and your profile.

Tips

  • Every tab has a ? help button in the top right with a detailed description of that tab.
  • Customize which variables appear on each surface via the ⚙ panels — your choices are personal and persist across sessions.
  • Charts stay synchronised when you zoom or pan in Timelines; click any point for that day's stats.
  • Other researchers' annotations appear in Video Analysis when they've opted into sharing.

Stuck, found a bug, or need access to a study? Email info@foryouresearch.net and we'll help you out.

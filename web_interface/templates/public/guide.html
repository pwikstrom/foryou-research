You're also welcome to try the analysis tools yourself. Sign up, follow the instructions below, and you will be able to use Explore, Video Analysis, Timelines, Correlations, and Semantic Space. Note that this is only a little peek at the Hub's analysis tools, running against a sample dataset we've made available to you. It's not the full pipeline, but it should give you a good feel for how the Data Hub works.

What you can explore here is a sample dataset we've opened up for a look around. Ingesting your own participants' donations and running the full ingest-to-insight pipeline isn't part of this preview. When you're ready for that, get in touch and we'll walk you through it.

Getting access

  1. Create an account. Go to the sign-up page and register with your email address, a username and a password.
  2. Wait for approval. New accounts are reviewed before they're enabled for the sample dataset. You'll see "pending approval" until your account is activated, it shouldn't take more than 24 hours.
  3. Log in. You'll land in the app with the analysis tabs — Explore, Timelines, Video Analysis, Correlations and Semantic Space — ready to use against the sample study. Ingestion and administration aren't part of this peek.

A short tour of the analysis tools

With the sample study loaded, here are some things you might try:

  1. Get an overview in Explore. Open the Explore tab to see how any variable is distributed across the study. Use the filter panel to slice the data — for example, videos from a particular content category, or plays longer than a threshold — and switch to two-slice mode to compare groups side by side. Significance stars flag differences between the slices.
  2. Follow a participant in Timelines. Pick a collection (one participant's donation) and watch how their feed composition evolves day by day. Filter chips isolate rising, falling, spiking or volatile categories, and "Show Findings" lists detected trends, anomalies and structural breaks.
  3. Drill into videos. From any chart in Explore or Correlations, click through to Video Analysis to watch the underlying videos, inspect their full metadata and AI annotations, and add your own tags and notes.
  4. Go deeper. Correlations charts relationships between numeric measures (with regression lines, group ellipses and a correlation heatmap), and Semantic Space maps every annotated video by meaning so you can explore the corpus's micro-genres. Use the zoom and navigation buttons in the Semantic Space to explore different regions of the mapping.

Tips

  • Every tab has a ? help button in the top right with a detailed description of that tab.
  • Customize which variables appear on each surface via the ⚙ panels. Your choices are personal and persist across sessions.
  • Other researchers' annotations appear in Video Analysis when they've opted into sharing.

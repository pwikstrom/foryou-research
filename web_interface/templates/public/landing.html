The For You Data Hub

A research platform for exploring social-media feeds donated by study participants — TikTok, Instagram and YouTube — built by The For You Research Project at QUT and the University of Sydney.

Log in How it works About the project

What is the Data Hub?

The Data Hub turns donated platform data into an interactive research environment. Study participants export their activity data from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube and donate it to the project; the hub enriches it and makes it explorable for the research team.

⇪

Donated data

Participants share their platform data exports — what they watched, when, for how long, and what they engaged with. The hub parses these donations into a common, platform-agnostic activity format.

✦

Enrichment

Each video in a donated feed is enriched with public metadata — captions, creators, popularity — and annotated by AI with content categories, themes, style and more, turning raw logs into analysable variables.

◫

Analysis

Interactive tools let researchers compare distributions, follow individual feeds over time, watch and code videos, chart correlations, and navigate a semantic map of the whole corpus.

What you can do

Explore the data

Compare variable distributions across the active study, optionally split into two filtered slices. Build a selection with checkboxes and range sliders, run two slices side by side, and let significance stars flag the differences. Click any bar to drill into the matching videos.

Follow feeds over time

Timelines shows how a single participant's feed evolves day by day. Every variable becomes a smoothed timeline with optional daily values, trend and anomaly detection, and filter chips that isolate rising, falling, spiking or volatile categories.

Watch and code videos

Video Analysis is the qualitative workbench: filter videos on any metadata variable, watch them in place, inspect the full enrichment record, and apply your own tags and notes — including shared team annotations.

Chart relationships

Correlations places one dot per collection-day on any two numeric axes, with group ellipses, regression lines and a full correlation heatmap — plus PCA-based measures computed per study.

Navigate the semantic space

Every annotated video is embedded by meaning and mapped in 2D: similar content sits close together, auto-named "niches" reveal micro-genres, and colouring by category or popularity shows how the corpus organises itself.

This tool is currently under development, so you may occasionally encounter bugs or unexpected behaviour. If you do, we'd really appreciate your help in improving it — please let us know at info@foryouresearch.net.

Some of the data and insights presented here are generated using AI. While we aim for accuracy, there may be instances where outputs are imperfect or unexpected. We appreciate your understanding as we continue to refine both the tool and our research methods.

From messy data donations to research findings

Data donation is a compelling way to study lived, in-the-wild platform experiences, but donated feeds are messy, stripped of metadata, and challenging to annotate at scale. The For You Data Hub closes the distance between a donation and a finding.

What is data donation? →

The For You Data Hub is a rigorous research platform for exploring short-video feeds donated by study participants. The Hub ingests data from TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts and is built by researchers in The For You Research Project based at QUT and the University of Sydney.

The Problem:
"Data donation is a compelling idea…
but the data is too messy to use."

Sometimes true, but it's almost never the data's fault. It's the distance between the donated data and the finding. Between a donation and an insight lies a mountain of data wrangling.

⇄

Reconcile formats

Endless stitching together of unpredictable, ever-changing platform export formats.

✦

Extend the donation

Videos, captions, authors, music and engagement counts are generally stripped out of donated packages and have to be recovered before analysis.

◱

Hand-code content

Thousands of items need to be coded, video by video, to become useful research variables.

Projects often stall before meaningful analysis can even begin.

The Hub automates the pipeline

The For You Data Hub takes over the wrangling and gives HASS researchers the tools to explore user experiences — from raw donation through to shareable analysis.

Ingest

Mixed formats and multiple participants become one clean, queryable corpus, grouped into studies.

Enrich

Recovers the stripped-out videos, captions, authors, music and engagement counts.

Annotate

AI turns each video into structured research variables, with human quality control.

Analyse

Time-series, statistics and qualitative video analysis, side by side.

Share

Role-based access brings collaborators and students in with the right permissions.

See it in action

Analyse

Compare across the corpus

Explore compares variable distributions across a study, split into two filtered slices with significance testing. Build selections with checkboxes and range sliders, and click any bar to drill into the underlying videos.

Analyse

Follow a feed over time

Timelines shows how a single participant's feed evolves day by day — smoothed per-variable timelines with trend, anomaly and structural-break detection, and filter chips for rising, falling, spiking or volatile categories.

Annotate

Watch and code in context

Video Analysis is the qualitative workbench: filter on any variable, watch videos in place, inspect the full enrichment and AI-annotation record, and code close to the source — solo or in teams, with inter-coder reliability.

Analyse

Find structure and patterns

Correlations places one dot per collection-day on any two numeric axes, with group ellipses, regression lines and a full correlation heatmap — plus PCA-based measures computed per study.

Annotate

Map the whole corpus

Scraped and annotated variables become 1536-dimensional embeddings, mapped into a readable 2D semantic space and grouped into fine-grained, auto-named "niches" — micro-genres you can navigate and colour by category or popularity.

Built for teams, running in the cloud

The Hub is a browser-based tool — nothing to install. Role-based access lets you bring collaborators and students in with the right permissions, and everything runs on managed cloud infrastructure that scales with your project.

Interface — browser-based, nothing to install Storage & compute — Google Cloud AI annotation — Google Gemini

What happens after you share

Instantly: your persona. The Hub reads your file and shows your short-video persona right away: viewing rhythms, archetype, cohort comparison.
Raw data first. Your activity history is in the Hub, but the videos themselves aren't analysed yet. That is the difference between raw and annotated data.
Then: scraping and annotation. We fetch and analyse the videos you watched: content, themes, style. This happens in batches over time, and your My Collections page always shows how far along your collection is.
Finally: explore for real. Explore your data and learn how it compares to other short-video users who have donated their data to the For You Project.

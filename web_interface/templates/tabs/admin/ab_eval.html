Contracts

An annotation contract is the recipe for how your videos get annotated: the questions the AI model is asked to answer about each video, and the form its answers must take. The table below lists every contract on this platform. Exactly one contract is active at any time — it is the one used whenever new annotations are made. To switch, press Activate on another contract: you will see a summary of what changes before anything is applied, and the activated contract (together with the AI model and its settings) is recorded as a new version on the Versions page. Use View to read any contract, or Duplicate to use it as the starting point for your own.

Contract evaluation

Before switching to a new contract, you can try it out here. Pick a small set of already-downloaded videos, run two or more contracts (or AI models) on the same videos side by side, and compare their answers field by field. Test runs make real annotation calls (contracts × videos), but the results are stored separately for comparison only — they never enter your research datasets.

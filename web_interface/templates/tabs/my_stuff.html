How to get your personal data from TikTok

Getting your data is super easy! Request it from TikTok, wait until it's ready to download (usually a few seconds, sometimes a few hours), download the file, and upload it here. You can do this in the TikTok app or in a browser at tiktok.com.

  1. In the TikTok app, tap Profile, open the menu (☰) and choose Settings and privacy — or on tiktok.com, click your profile picture and choose Settings.
  2. Go to Account → Download your data (on the website, look under Privacy).
  3. Select All data (or at least your Activity) and choose JSON as the file format, then tap Request data.
  4. When your file appears under the Download data tab, download the .zip.
  5. Unzip it and upload the user_data_tiktok.json inside. You review everything before it is shared.

How to get your personal data from Instagram

Instagram data is requested through Meta's Accounts Centre. It usually takes from a few minutes up to a day before your file is ready.

  1. In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap the menu (☰), then Your activity → Download your information — or go to Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions in a browser.
  2. Choose Download or transfer information and select your Instagram account.
  3. Pick All available information (simplest), or "Some of your information" with at least Likes, Story interactions and Ads information selected.
  4. Choose Download to device, set Date range to All time and Format to JSON, then create the file.
  5. Instagram notifies you when the .zip is ready. Download it and upload it here as-is — no need to unzip. Only the activity files are read, and you review everything before it is shared.

How to get your personal data from YouTube

YouTube data comes through Google Takeout. The export usually arrives within minutes, occasionally hours.

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account you use for YouTube.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick only YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Click All YouTube data included and keep at least history, comments and playlists.
  4. Click Multiple formats and set History to JSON.
  5. Continue with Export once and create the export. Google emails you a download link when it is ready.
  6. Download the .zip and upload it here as-is — no need to unzip. Only your watch history and activity files are read, and you review everything before it is shared.

Consent for participating in the For You Research Project

Before you decide if you are willing to share your data, you must be well informed about the purpose of the study and what your participation involves:

Why is the study being conducted?

This project examines how automated content recommendation systems (such as TikTok) affect everyday users of media platforms. It explores how these systems shape what we see online, how different audiences understand and engage with them, and their impact on short-video creators.

Who benefits from the results of this research?

The findings from this research support policymakers in designing policies and regulations for digital platforms and broader government priorities on automation and AI technologies. The project also helps content creators understand and adapt to platform policies and strategies and make better career decisions.

What are the possible benefits for you if you take part?

By participating in this project, you will gain a new perspective on how you use short-video platforms and learn about the personal data platforms store about you.

What does your participation involve?

You have already requested and received your personal data from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube and used this website to review which data items you want to share. Now, you are at the stage where you upload your data file to the research team. Your participation in this research project is entirely voluntary. Even if you initially agree to participate, you can withdraw from the research project without comment or penalty. If you withdraw, on request, any information already obtained that can be linked to you will be destroyed. Your decision to participate or not participate will not impact your current or future relationship with QUT.

What are the possible risks for you if you take part?

There are no risks beyond day-to-day living associated with your participation in this research project.

What about privacy and confidentiality?

The data you share is anonymised, and the research team will not link your real-world identity with your data. Any data collected for this research project will be stored securely per QUT’s data management policies for a minimum of five years. It can be disclosed to protect you or others from harm if specifically required by law or if a regulatory or monitoring body such as the QUT Human Research Ethics Committee requests it.

How do I give my consent to participate?

You will consent to participate by actively sharing your data via this website.

What if I have questions about the research project?

If you have any questions or require further information, please get in touch with the research team at foryou@qut.edu.au or info@foryouresearch.net.

What if I have a concern or complaint regarding the conduct of the research project?

QUT is committed to research integrity and the ethical conduct of research projects. If you wish to discuss the study with someone not directly involved, particularly about matters concerning policies, information or complaints about the conduct of the study or your rights as a participant, you may contact the QUT Research Ethics Advisory Team on +61 (0)7 3138 5123 or email humanethics@qut.edu.au, stating the project’s QUT Ethics Approval Number LR 2024-8002-21442. Thank you for helping with this research project.
